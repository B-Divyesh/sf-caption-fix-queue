Plain-language policy

Privacy

The checker processes caption text in your browser. It does not upload captions to Caption Fix Queue or an AI service.

What stays local

Imported captions, edits, glossary terms, decisions, and review history use this browser’s local storage (IndexedDB). Your Studio license uses local storage too. Export your data or delete the current workspace from the document menu.

What leaves your device

The free checker sends no caption, glossary, or review-history content. Buying or verifying Studio contacts the Sociobot billing API. Only the license token is sent for verification. Sociobot/Dodo handles payment and provides its privacy notice at checkout.

Service worker and logs

The app caches interface files for offline use after the first visit. The host may keep short security logs such as an IP address and path. The app has no analytics, ads, tracking scripts, or services that load code or fonts from other sites.

Your choices

Use the export tools to keep a downloaded copy. “Delete local workspace” erases the current captions and history. Clearing this site’s browser data removes all work and the stored license.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
