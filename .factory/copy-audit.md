## Action and heading labels

Labels use verbs where they cause an action: “Try it with sample data”, “Choose
a caption file”, “Review findings”, “Export repairs”, “Use dark theme”, “View
Studio options”, “Reset demo”, and “Start for real”. Headings name their content
without relying on surrounding text.
