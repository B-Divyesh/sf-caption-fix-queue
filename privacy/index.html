Plain-language policy

Privacy

Your caption text belongs to you. The checker parses and stores it on your device; it is not sent to Caption Fix Queue or an AI service.

What stays local

Imported captions, edits, glossary terms, finding decisions, and review history are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. Your Studio license token is stored in localStorage. You can export your data or delete the local workspace from the document menu.

What leaves your device

Nothing leaves your device while using the free checker. If you buy or verify Studio, your browser contacts the Sociobot billing API. The license token is sent for verification; caption text and glossary data are not. Hosted checkout is operated by Sociobot/Dodo as merchant of record and is covered by its checkout privacy notice.

Service worker and logs

The app caches its interface files so it can work offline. The hosting provider may retain short-lived security logs such as an IP address and requested path. Caption Fix Queue includes no behavioral analytics, advertising pixels, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts.

Your choices

Use the export tools to keep a portable copy. Use “Delete local workspace” to erase the current transcript and history. You may also clear this site’s browser storage to remove all local data and a stored license.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
